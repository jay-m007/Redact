Not the secrets.

Redact finds Aadhaar, PAN, phone numbers, UPI IDs and more in a picture, covers them with solid black, and removes the hidden location and phone details. It all happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop, paste or choose a picture

Screenshots and photos of documents. JPEG, PNG or WebP.

What it hides

Aadhaar (checked with its own checksum), PAN, phone and mobile numbers, email addresses, UPI IDs, card numbers, bank account numbers, IFSC codes, dates of birth, names after a label like Name:, OTPs and CVVs, vehicle numbers, passports, voter IDs and driving licences. You can also draw a box over anything else: a face, an address, a signature.

Always look at the result before you share it. Reading text from a picture is never perfect, and Redact cannot recognise a name or address that has no label next to it.

Drag on the picture to draw a black box. Tap a box to remove it.
